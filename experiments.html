Experiment Demos

Each demo below is a simplified version of a real experiment from my research. They're designed to give you a hands-on feel for the tasks participants complete — and a sense of what it's like to be a research subject. Click a tile to get started.

Available Demos

Team Spirit: teaming with a machine partner

Keep the balls in play alongside a machine teammate while answering a second task, and watch your miss rate climb as the workload rises. A short single-player version of the published study.

Wheel of Fortune

Identify numerals buried in noise — Arabic, Chinese, Thai or dots — then watch your mistakes turn into a map of how close those symbols sit in your head.

Scaling lesion features by comparison

How irregular is irregular? Compare pairs of skin lesions on one feature, and watch a Bradley–Terry–Luce model turn your choices into a measured scale.

Choice task vs rating scale

Pick the darkest of three squares, or rate your confidence that a marked one is darkest — two ways of asking the same question, and a test of whether they read the same judgment.
