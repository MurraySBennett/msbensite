My research focuses on decision-making under uncertainty in applied settings, particularly where human judgement interacts with intelligent systems. Using formal cognitive models and computational simulations, I study perceptual integration, workload, and metacognition in tasks such as medical image interpretation and collaborative decision-making. A central goal of my work is to identify principled ways to improve performance in human–AI teams.
