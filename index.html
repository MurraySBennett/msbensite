I am a computational cognitive scientist. I use mathematical models, behavioural experiments and machine learning to study perception, workload capacity and decision-making in people and teams.

My current work looks at how AI systems can report their own uncertainty, and how people decide when to rely on them. I also build methods and software for testing cognitive theories. Two of these, GRIN and KITH, run in a web browser.

Research areas

Human-AI collaboration

How people use an AI system's uncertainty when deciding whether to trust it, and what it costs a person to coordinate with an AI teammate. I study this with controlled experiments and models of team performance.

Cognitive models

Building and testing mathematical models of how people process information, and methods for fitting those models to data.

Perception

How people judge individual and joint perceptual features. Examples include skin lesion images and unfamiliar numerals.
