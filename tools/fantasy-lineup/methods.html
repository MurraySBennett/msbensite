Methods and evidence

This is an unlisted experiment. No admissible real historical week is available for a rolling backtest, so the model has not passed a baseline or probability calibration check. Point ranges describe raw simulated draws; they are not validated coverage claims. P(A > B) is withheld until calibration.

Inputs and forecast

Licensed public NFL data are frozen at a stated cutoff. A seeded generative model draws team opportunity, player share, availability and scoring components together, with partial pooling for players with little history. Each player's usage estimate combines at most three earlier games with two position-prior games. Team opportunities are shared across teammates within each draw. Kicker attempts and makes use separate components. These assumptions are documented in the source repository's fantasy/model.md; the preview branch is not published yet.

Availability assumptions are: out 0, doubtful 25%, questionable 75%, probable 90%, and unknown 90%. These numbers are model priors, not measured injury probabilities. Unknown status remains visibly unknown.

Decision and limits

Your league scoring coefficients turn the same stat draws into points for every legal lineup. The tool chooses the lineup with the highest modeled mean points. D/ST can be fixed by you, but its points are excluded. Bye players and already started games cannot enter a new recommendation. Missing injury reports appear as unknown; they do not mean healthy.

“What if?” changes a stated assumption inside the model and re-optimizes. It is conditional sensitivity, not evidence that the change will occur or cause an outcome. It never replaces your frozen official advice.

Privacy and results

Roster, scoring, picks and probabilities remain in this browser's local storage. Export includes names, player IDs, rules, picks, probabilities, reasons and source times; keep it private. A separate, versioned outcome snapshot supplies observed player statistics. Missing rows leave a week pending; they are never treated as zero. A corrected outcome version changes realized scores while frozen pregame picks remain intact.

Results separates three quantities: realized fantasy points under your exact scoring, forecast error from frozen expected player points, and personal-pilot inference. User-fixed D/ST is excluded from modeled totals. The hindsight oracle is the best legal lineup from the same frozen roster under observed points; its regret is a retrospective benchmark, not a strategy that was available before kickoff. A late final revision remains visible, but the earlier prelock final is used for prospective comparison. A choice disclosed as externally informed still receives a score but is excluded from the unaided comparison.

The personal-pilot summary reports a paired final-minus-model mean only for eligible completed weeks. A descriptive 95% bootstrap interval appears at eight such weeks. Final comparison-probability calibration bins appear only with at least 20 predictions and at least five in each of three bins. Small samples show “too few”; no population effect is inferred.

Evidence status

The reproducible check found zero archived issue-time historical weeks and zero reviewed real outcome snapshots for comparison. A one-week synthetic fixture exercises four forecast players, three paired baseline scores and one distinct synthetic legal roster; its model point error is 8.921 versus 7.033 for the rolling baseline. Those invented outcomes cannot establish predictive accuracy or a model advantage. Real interval coverage, calibration, lineup regret and scenario coverage remain unmeasured. The full counts, source requirements, feature-status table and release gates are in the repository's fantasy/evidence.md.

The route stays unlisted and noindex. A real-data rolling comparison, end-to-end reviewed update and a short scenario-wording comprehension check with another reader are required before public promotion. No superiority claim is made.
