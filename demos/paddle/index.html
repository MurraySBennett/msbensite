The paddle task

This is the actual experiment client — the same physics, timing, scoring and rendering participants get, running in your browser with no server behind it. Pick your settings and play a trial.

What you're doing

Balls fall from the top of the screen. Move your paddle to hit as many as you can before the trial ends.

What this demo can't show

The experiment's main manipulation is coordination structure — how scoring works between two players:

SeparateEach player hits only their own balls. Scored individually.
CollaborativeBoth hit all balls. Scored as a team total.
CompetitiveBoth hit all balls. Scored individually, and each can see the other's score.

All three need a second player, so none can be demoed alone. What you play here is the separate condition with one player — which in the experiment is the baseline that establishes alone performance before any group comparison is made.

Nothing you do here is recorded or leaves your browser.
