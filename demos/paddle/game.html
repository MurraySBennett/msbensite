Welcome
Team Spirit

This experiment examines your performance during a multiplayer arcade-style game.

The game involves balls flying around the screen and two paddles — one for you, one for Player 2.

Your task is to move your paddle to hit as many balls as you can, whilst collaborating, competing, or playing separately.

The game runs over 3 blocks of 12 trials, each trial lasting 45 seconds.

There is a short break between blocks. The experiment takes approximately 35–40 minutes.

Please ensure your browser window is maximised before continuing.
