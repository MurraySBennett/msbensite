Research

How do people combine information, recognise patterns and make decisions under uncertainty? I study these questions through behavioural experiments and mathematical models. The aim is to explain the processes behind a response, as well as to measure how accurately or quickly it was made.

My work connects workload capacity in teams, perceptual measurement and computational methods for testing cognitive theories. Human–AI collaboration provides one setting for these questions: how does coordination affect performance, and what information helps a person judge an AI prediction?

The projects below describe published findings, current manuscripts and research software. Each page sets out the question, the approach and what the evidence currently supports.
