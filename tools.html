GRIN: General Recognition Inference Network

Available

A free online tool that fits General Recognition Theory to identification data in milliseconds, with an uncertainty range for every estimate. It shows each result beside a standard fit and reports how well it recovers known values in simulated data. It runs in the browser, Python and R.

KITH: K-nearest index of typicality in hidden layers

Available

A demonstration of KITH, a general method that lets an AI model judge how familiar it is with your query. We use melanoma identification as the demonstration. Choose a close-up photo of a skin lesion, or take your own, to see the model's prediction along with a late (decisional) and an early (perceptual) score showing how familiar your image is compared with what the model has seen before. All images stay on your computer and nothing you enter is recorded. This is a research demonstration only and is positively not medical advice. We are not that kind of Doctor.
