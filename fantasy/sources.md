# Fantasy pilot source register

The offline updater captures four CSV assets from [nflverse-data releases](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases), whose repository declares [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Attribute nflverse and its source projects in every published Methods/Evidence view. Check individual upstream feeds again before public publication or a change in use. No FantasyPros or Boris Chen forecast is ingested.

| Local capture | Release asset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `stats_player_week_<season>.csv` | `stats_player/stats_player_week_<season>.csv` | Prior regular-season player stat components, joined by `player_id`. [Dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_player_stats.html). |
| `roster_weekly_<season>.csv` | `weekly_rosters/roster_weekly_<season>.csv` | Latest captured active-roster players, including those without prior stats, joined by `gsis_id`. [Status dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_roster_status.html). |
| `injuries_<season>.csv` | `injuries/injuries_<season>.csv` | Reported injury context, joined by `gsis_id`. [Dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_injuries.html). |
| `games.csv` | `schedules/games.csv` | Week, opponents and announced game time. |

`fantasy.source.capture_sources` stores the exact URL, retrieval time, SHA-256 and licence for each raw download. `load_week` rejects any capture after the requested cutoff and filters out target-week stat outcomes. The current 2026 injury CSV **does not contain `date_modified`**, although the published injury dictionary lists it. For that feed, the retrieval timestamp is the earliest defensible time at which this copy's status was known. A later download cannot reconstruct a historical pre-lock injury report, so retrospective injury-feature tests are disallowed without a dated archive. Absence from this feed means *unknown*, not healthy. A pre-lock snapshot must be captured before the first game it covers and refreshed ahead of later games if their statuses change.

The upcoming week's roster rows may not exist yet. `load_week` uses the latest available roster week at or before the target week and reports it as `roster_week`; downstream advice must show when it is stale. `ACT` means on the team's active roster, **not** confirmed active for game day. Historical stat rows alone never make a player eligible. Roster changes after capture and individual game-day inactives remain unknown until a newer source is captured.

Release files can be corrected after a game. A backtest using today's prior-week stat files is an approximation to issue-time input, and its provenance limitation must appear in results. The prospective snapshots generated by this project are the stronger evidence. Schedule rows are reduced to opponent and kickoff; final scores and retrospective weather never enter `WeekInput`.

Schema smoke check, 2026-09-30: the four 2026 release CSVs were captured at 18:44:47–18:44:48 UTC and joined for week 5 with a cutoff of 18:47:44 UTC. No week-5 roster rows existed, so the week-4 `ACT` roster supplied 536 QB/RB/WR/TE/K IDs; 502 had a scheduled week-5 game, 1,126 prior-week stat rows were retained for those IDs, and no week-5 injury rows were available. These are source-schema counts, not a claim that all 536 will be game-day active or forecastable. No download from this check was shipped or committed.
